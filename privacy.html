lemma-md

Privacy policy

lemma-md is a browser app for reading and writing Markdown with TeX. It runs entirely in your browser. This page describes what it does with your data — which is, by design, as little as possible.

The short version

There is no server and no account. Your notes are kept in your own browser and are uploaded nowhere unless you explicitly ask to save them to Google Drive. We do not run analytics, set tracking cookies, or collect personal information. We never see your notes.

Notes stored in your browser

Notes you write, and any drawings you make when presenting, are saved locally in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) on the device you are using. They stay on that device. Clearing your browser's site data for this origin removes them, so keep a copy — download it, save it to disk, or save it to Google Drive — if it matters to you.

Because this storage belongs to your browser, it is not shared with us or with anyone else, and we have no way to read it.

Google Drive (only if you connect)

Connecting to Google Drive is optional. If you never connect, none of this applies and Google's libraries are never loaded.

When you choose to connect, the app requests a single, narrow permission — the drive.file scope. This means the app can only access files that you pick through its file chooser, or that it creates itself. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.

File contents travel directly between your browser and Google. There is no server of ours in between, and your files never pass through us.

The access token that authorises these requests is held in memory only, for the duration of the tab. It is never written to disk or to browser storage, and closing the tab ends the session. No refresh token is stored.

To make connecting and the file chooser work, Google's own JavaScript libraries are loaded from Google's servers at the moment you connect, and your requests to Google are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

lemma-md's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we do not do

  • We do not have a server that receives your notes.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising, and set no tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone.
  • We do not use your Drive data to train models or for any purpose other than the file operations you ask for.
