Troubleshooting

When something does not work as it should: what the message means, and how to put it right.

“This browser won’t let lemma-md store anything”

No browser blocks this by default. The message means the browser was set to stop sites from keeping data on your device: in its settings, by an extension, or by whoever manages the computer. Usually it comes with blocking all cookies, because browsers treat a site’s storage like its cookies.

Until it is allowed, lemma-md still works but keeps nothing, so save or download each note before you close the tab. Allowing lemma-md alone is enough; the rest of the web can stay blocked. When you have changed the setting, reload the lemma-md page — after saving your notes, since while storage is blocked a reload loses them.

The site to allow is lemma-md.github.io.

Chrome

  1. Open Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → Additional content settings → On-device site data, or type chrome://settings/content/siteData into the address bar.
  2. For lemma-md alone: next to Allowed to save data on your device, click Add and enter the site.
  3. Or, for every site: under Default behavior, choose Allow sites to save data on your device.

If your notes vanish each time you quit Chrome, the same page has Delete data sites have saved to your device when you close all windows selected. Adding lemma-md to the allowed sites fixes that too.

Edge

  1. Open Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data, or type edge://settings/content/cookies into the address bar.
  2. For lemma-md alone: next to Allow, click Add and enter the site.
  3. Or, for every site: turn on Allow sites to save and read cookie data.

Firefox

  1. Open Settings → Privacy & Security, and under Cookies and Site Data click Manage Exceptions….
  2. Enter the site, click Allow, then Save Changes.

An exception also keeps your notes when Delete cookies and site data when Firefox is closed is on.

Safari

  1. On a Mac: Safari → Settings → Privacy, and turn off Block all cookies.
  2. On an iPhone or iPad: in the Settings app, open Safari (under Apps on recent versions) and turn off Block All Cookies.

Safari has no exception for a single site.

If that is not it

  • A setting that is greyed out, or marked as managed by your organisation, can be changed only by whoever manages the computer.
  • A privacy or cookie-blocking extension can do the same. Allow lemma-md in it, or try a window where extensions are off.
