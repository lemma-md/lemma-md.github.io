What you can do

Share math as plain text

AI already writes math this way: Markdown with LaTeX, which survives any email or message. lemma-md typesets it for you and for anyone you send it to — no plugin, no app, no account.

Write TeX, no compile

Type on the left; the page on the right follows as you go, formulas included. Close the tab whenever you like — the note is still here when you come back.

Write slides like notes

Start a note with a line of --- and it becomes slides; each further --- starts the next one. Same Markdown, same formulas, and a title and author at the top make the title slide.

Give the talk

Present puts a note on the projector, as slides or one page you scroll. Laser and pen to mark it up, and a blackboard with chalk for what does not fit on a slide — or for a talk without slides.

Review your board

An answer to a question, an idea worked out on the spot: what you write during a talk is kept, on the slides and on every page of the board, to go through again afterwards.

Save it, send it, print it

Keep notes in a file on your computer or on Google Drive. Send one as .md, as .tex or as a web page anyone can open; export a talk to PDF, chalk and board included.

Everything runs in your browser. Your notes stay on your machine and go nowhere unless you save them to Google Drive.
