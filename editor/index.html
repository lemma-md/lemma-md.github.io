Settings

Where your notes are kept

Notes are stored in this browser, on this computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes them, and they will not appear on your other devices.

Bin

A note you close with Discard changes comes here, with the changes you discarded. Restore it to a tab, or leave it — the bin empties itself after a while.

    Cloud

    Notes kept in a cloud drive survive this browser and reach your other devices. Files are opened and saved through the provider's own chooser — this app only ever sees the files you pick.
